What it does

Research

Run metered keyword research with dated volume, difficulty, CPC, related-search, and question signals when available.

Review and publish

Queue one keyword and angle, inspect the draft, then publish or schedule to a verified domain.

Measure

Read Google Search Console performance, first-party sessions, crawler activity, and sampled AI citations.

Evidence

The workflow keeps the query, angle, draft state, live URL, measurement window, citation evidence, and unavailable fields attached to the work.

Where the data comes from

  • Metered DataForSEO-backed keyword research and returned Google-related question signals.
  • Google Search Console clicks, impressions, and positions.
  • Infinite first-party sessions and verified crawler activity.
  • Repeated samples across ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI Overview.

Review boundary

Queueing defaults to a held draft. Publishing from Cmd+L requires confirmation; Review first keeps every draft waiting. Autopublish is an explicit founder choice, and campaign timing still follows release controls.

Limitations

AEO is sampled visibility, not a census. Research is metered and capped. Publishing needs a verified domain. Infinite does not promise rankings or citations, and unserved measurements remain unmeasured rather than zero.
