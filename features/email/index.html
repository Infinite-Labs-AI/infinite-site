Current availability

Email workflows are not yet a public Infinite product

The repository retains useful email infrastructure. This page records what exists and what must ship before founders are asked to trust it.

See shipped workflows

What exists

Audience infrastructure

Contacts, lists, dynamic segments, consent fields, and Shopify customer/order backfill remain in the backend.

Draft infrastructure

Template preview, brand-grounded copy generation, campaign drafts, sequence definitions, workers, metrics, and unsubscribe handling exist.

Operator groundwork

Intelligence scans can persist proposed actions, while scheduled scanning and v1 auto-execution remain paused or incomplete.

What is not public yet

The customer-facing email dashboard and coordinator are not public product surfaces. Infinite does not currently advertise campaign sending, sequence activation, inbox automation, or a deliverability guarantee as a founder-ready feature.

Launch prerequisites

Launch requires a current customer surface that shows visible sender and domain state, audience and recipient count, content preview, unsubscribe and compliance state, send time and timezone, and activation confirmation before a send-affecting action.
