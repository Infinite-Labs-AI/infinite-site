# Infinite Max + Ultra Pricing Design

## Goal

Create a reviewable visual comparison of three genuinely different pricing-section directions for Infinite Max and Infinite Ultra. The selected direction will later replace the homepage's existing single-plan pricing section.

## Commercial content

- Infinite Max costs $49 per month.
- Infinite Ultra costs $199 per month.
- Both plans include a 7-day free trial and can be cancelled at any time.
- Infinite Ultra receives stronger visual emphasis.
- Max retains all capabilities in the current $49 plan.
- Ultra includes everything in Max, plus:
  - AI Visibility
  - Reels
  - Competitor Tracking across content, pricing, and ads
- The interface includes Monthly and Annual billing controls.
- Until annual prices are supplied, Monthly is the active state and Annual is visibly labelled "Pricing coming soon." The prototype must not imply or invent an annual discount.

## Review artifact

Build one self-contained review page that uses the current Infinite homepage's typography, light background, dark CTA, green confirmation color, rounded cards, and subtle blue/pink edge glow. It must not modify the live homepage pricing section during this design-review stage.

The review page presents all three directions with clear labels, short rationale, desktop-responsive layouts, and working billing controls. Each direction includes a small reaction panel with three fields: choose this direction, details to steal, and details to reject. A generated summary at the end turns those reactions into a concise combined-direction brief the user can copy back into the conversation.

## Direction 1: Twin plan cards

Use two directly comparable plan cards. Max is a calm white card; Ultra is elevated with stronger contrast, a subtle dark or spectral surface, and a "Most powerful" label. Each card includes plan name, positioning line, trial, monthly price, CTA, cancellation reassurance, and scoped feature list.

Intent: make plan choice immediately legible and keep the conversion path conventional.

Strengths: fastest comparison, clearest pricing, strongest conventional conversion hierarchy, easiest responsive behavior.

Trade-off: feature repetition can make the section taller.

## Direction 2: Interactive plan switcher

Use a single large pricing surface with Max and Ultra selector controls. Switching plans updates the price, visual treatment, CTA context, and feature content. Ultra reveals a distinct expansion region containing its three exclusive capability groups.

Intent: focus attention on one plan at a time and make Ultra feel like a richer product state.

Strengths: compact, immersive, visually distinctive, good storytelling opportunity.

Trade-off: visitors need interaction to compare every detail.

## Direction 3: Base + power-up

Present Max as the complete core product and attach an Ultra expansion panel headed "Everything in Max, plus." The expansion panel contains the three Ultra-exclusive capability groups and the Ultra price and CTA.

Intent: make the $150 price difference feel attached to concrete additional outcomes.

Strengths: clearest upgrade narrative, minimal duplicated copy, emphasizes Ultra's incremental value.

Trade-off: Ultra can read as an add-on if the hierarchy is not carefully balanced.

## Interaction behavior

- Monthly is selected by default.
- Selecting Annual keeps both plans visible but replaces numeric prices with an honest "Annual pricing coming soon" state.
- Returning to Monthly restores $49/month and $199/month.
- All controls must work with mouse, touch, and keyboard and expose accessible labels and selected state.
- Calls to action are visual-only in the design artifact and do not start checkout or download flows.
- Reaction controls persist only for the current browser session; no account or server storage is required.

## Responsive behavior

- Twin cards stack with Ultra first on narrow screens.
- The interactive switcher keeps the plan selector above the active panel.
- Base + power-up stacks as one connected vertical composition, preserving the "everything in Max, plus" relationship.
- Prices, feature text, and CTAs remain readable without horizontal scrolling.

## Success criteria

- The three directions differ in layout logic, information density, and interaction model rather than only color.
- Max remains credible and complete while Ultra is unmistakably emphasized.
- The Ultra-only capabilities are understandable within a short scan.
- No annual price or discount is fabricated.
- The user can select a preferred direction or produce a combined-direction brief from the review page.

## Recommended production direction

Twin plan cards are the leading recommendation because they make a two-tier decision easiest to understand and provide the least friction at the point of conversion. The review artifact deliberately includes the other two directions so useful interaction and upgrade-story details can be combined into the final production design.
