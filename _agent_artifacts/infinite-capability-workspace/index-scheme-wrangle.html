Frequently asked questions

Is Infinite an app, an agent, or a service?

Infinite is a desktop growth app with agents inside it. It connects to the tools you already use, watches for customer-acquisition signal, and prepares work for you to approve.

Why does Infinite request access to my Google account?

Infinite, by Ultima AI, Inc., connects the Google services you choose to use. Google Analytics 4 access lets Infinite show your selected property's traffic, acquisition channels, and conversions, and configure the conversion events declared for your workspace. Search Console access provides search performance for SEO reporting. Connecting YouTube lets you publish approved videos and add captions or comments; connecting Google Ads supports campaign reporting and management. Each integration asks for its own permissions when you connect it.

What does Infinite do first?

It starts by finding high-intent leads, SEO/GEO gaps, landing-page conversion issues, and organic content angles. The goal is to build your first customer pipeline before adding more automation.

How do Infinite OS action confirmations work?

Infinite OS's built-in/native action registry exposes typed actions. Live or destructive native actions pass native authority and policy checks and require operator confirmation before execution. Scoped app/MCP tools follow a separate boundary: the operator-authorized host supplies their schemas and owns their semantic validation and confirmation. A scoped tool or proposal can be invoked before the host or tool result requests follow-up confirmation. The built-in/native catalog has no arbitrary shell or code runner; a host may intentionally expose broader capabilities through scoped tools.

Where do my data, credentials, and model prompts go?

Infinite supports local engine workflows and hosted workspace features. The desktop's hosted Google Analytics connection synchronizes authorized metrics and encrypted credentials in Infinite's cloud. Local engine workflows can store data on your device. Prompts and relevant tool context go to the model provider you choose when you request AI assistance. See our Privacy Policy for the processing boundaries.

Is this for pre-revenue founders?

Yes, especially if you can build product but need help finding the first repeatable customer-acquisition motion. Infinite is meant to turn market signal into practical next moves.

How is this different from hiring a marketer?

A marketer brings judgment and taste. Infinite is the always-on operating layer: it watches sources, prepares drafts, keeps context together, and gives a founder or marketer better work to review.
